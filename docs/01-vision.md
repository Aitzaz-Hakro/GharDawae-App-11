# Ghar Dawae — Phase 1: Vision & Scope

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

## Core Problem
Families lose paper prescriptions and forget medical history. When a family
member is outside the home and needs an urgent medicine, they don't have the
prescription/receipt with them — and often no internet either.

## Who Hurts Most
1. **Me personally** — managing my own + family's scattered records.
2. **The family caretaker** — the one person caring for a sick family member,
   who has to remember everyone's medicines, doctors, and history.

## One-Line Pitch
> Ghar Dawae is an app that manages your family's medicine records for you —
> available even offline.

## What It Is NOT (v1 scope guards)
- **NOT** a doctor-recommendation / find-nearby-doctor app (deferred to later,
  possibly a future version — not now).
- **NOT** a medical-advice or diagnosis tool.

## Success Definition
- **Primary target:** 100 families actively using the app.
- More families = more success. Growth is the signal.

## Key Differentiator (early read)
- **Offline-first** access to prescriptions/medicines is the standout need
  (urgent-medicine-outside-home case). This is the feature to nail.
- Local-market fit (name is Urdu — "home medicine") suggests Pakistan / South
  Asia as the natural first audience. _To confirm in Phase 2._

## Open Questions Carried Forward
- Geography: confirm Pakistan/South Asia first vs global (Phase 2).
- Who "owns" the family account and who can view whose records (Phase 8).
- How receipts get in: photo scan vs manual entry vs both (Phase 3/6).
