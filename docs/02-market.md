# Ghar Dawae — Phase 2: Users & Market

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

## Goal Type
**(B) Real product to grow** — aim for market share, not just portfolio.
This raises the bar: wedge must be sharp and defensible against funded incumbents.

## Geography & Language
- **Market:** Pakistan only (v1).
- **Language:** English UI. Content/data can be English + Urdu (e.g. Urdu
  prescriptions, Urdu search terms later). UI stays English for v1.

## Account Model (early)
- **Every member signs up** (own login/identity).
- **One member is the manager** — manages the family, and pays if the app has
  charges. (Detailed roles/permissions decided in Phase 8.)

## Personas (2)
### Persona 1 — The Manager (primary = the user)
Tech-comfortable family member who adds and organizes records for everyone.
Owns the family account, handles any payment. Wants control + full history.

### Persona 2 — The Caretaker
The person looking after a sick or elderly family member. Needs fast, reliable
access to that member's meds and prescriptions — often urgently, often offline.
(Manager and Caretaker are frequently the SAME person.)

## Competitive Landscape (honest)
Pakistani market is NOT empty. Key players:

| App | Focus | Threat level |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| [oladoc](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/oladoc-the-health-app/id1343718442) | Doctor booking + labs + meds + records (records secondary) | High reach, but records are an afterthought |
| [Marham](https://www.marham.pk/) | Telemedicine, verified doctors, booking | Booking-first, records secondary |
| [MyHealth Rex](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arkaagp.medirecords) | Records-first, offline, on-device, encryption, AI, family care | **Closest competitor — near-identical pitch** |

**Reality:** "Offline" and "Pakistani" are NOT unique anymore (Rex claims both).
The bet cannot be tech alone — it must be **positioning + focus**.

## The Wedge (the one bet)
> **Ghar Dawae = the family caretaker's medicine app.**
> One person manages the whole family's medicines + prescriptions. Offline.
> Dead simple. Built for the person who carries everyone's health in their head.

Why defensible:
1. **Family-caretaker structure** — incumbents are individual-account,
   booking-first. Rebuilding around a family-caretaker contradicts their
   business model; they won't chase it.
2. **Emotional loyalty** — caretakers are high-stakes users; strong
   word-of-mouth (organic growth).
3. **Simplicity as a moat** — big apps are bloated; they can't strip down
   without hurting revenue. We can.
