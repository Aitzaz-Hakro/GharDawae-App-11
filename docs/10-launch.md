# Ghar Dawae — Phase 10: Launch & Growth

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

## Decisions
- **Beta testers:** yes — own family first, then 3-5 friendly families.
- **Distribution:** **Google Play only** for v1 (Pakistan = Android-first). iOS deferred.
- **Monetization:** **Free v1.** Grow to 100 families first; revenue later.
- **Growth channels:** word-of-mouth + social media.

## 1. Beta Test Plan
- **Phase A — own family:** real data, real usage, catch the obvious breaks.
- **Phase B — 3-5 friendly families:** honest feedback, watch the offline
  3-tap journey and fast-entry UX in real hands.
- Watch for: does manual entry feel fast? does offline actually work when they
  need it? do they trust it with medical data?

## 2. Distribution — Google Play
- **Google Play Console** — one-time ~$25 developer fee.
- Android-only v1 (Pakistan iPhone share small; iOS deferred).
- Needs: app listing (English), screenshots, description, **privacy policy URL**
  (from Phase 8 — required by Play for health data).
- Health-data apps get extra Play review scrutiny → declare data handling
  honestly in the Data Safety form.

## 3. Monetization — Free v1
- Free to grow. Revenue deferred.
- **Future paid ideas (post-100 families):** cloud backup storage tiers,
  v2 AI assistant as premium, multi-manager family sharing.
- Manager = the payer when charges arrive (from Phase 2).

## 4. Growth to 100 Families
- **Word-of-mouth (primary)** — caretakers talk to caretakers. Your wedge is
  emotional/high-trust → strong organic referral. Make sharing easy
  ("invite a family" later).
- **Social media** — Pakistan channels: WhatsApp family groups, Facebook groups,
  Instagram. Short demo videos of the 3-tap offline lookup = the hook.
- **Sequence:** own family → their networks → social posts showing the core
  offline value → iterate on what resonates.

## 5. Feedback Loop
- Simple channel v1: a WhatsApp number or in-app "send feedback" link.
- Track: crashes, sync failures, entry friction, feature requests.
- Feed the top pains back into the build (esp. M2 sync + M3 entry).

## Launch Checklist (v1)
- [ ] App tested on real low-end Android (Pakistan target device)
- [ ] Offline 3-tap journey verified in airplane mode
- [ ] RLS cross-family isolation verified
- [ ] Privacy policy hosted (URL for Play)
- [ ] Play Console account + Data Safety form
- [ ] Store listing: screenshots, English description, demo video
- [ ] Own family using it (Phase A) before public
- [ ] Feedback channel live

## Success Metric (from Phase 1)
- **100 families actively using = success.** More = more success.

## Post-Launch → v2
- AI assistant (Phase 5 spec), medicine reminders.
- Then "Later": doctor recommendation.
